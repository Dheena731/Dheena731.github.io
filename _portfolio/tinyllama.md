**2024 · PEFT and LoRA**

Fine-tuned TinyLLaMA on the CUAD legal-contract dataset using LoRA adapters and Kaggle GPUs, with Weights & Biases experiment tracking.

The recorded project results were **1.1219 validation loss** and **1.0250 training loss** after **2,800 steps**.

**Technologies:** Python, PyTorch, Transformers, PEFT, LoRA, Weights & Biases.
